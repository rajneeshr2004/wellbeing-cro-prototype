The science, plainly.

These studies looked at collagen peptides as an ingredient, not at this specific product.

What was measured

Trials mostly measured skin hydration and elasticity with instruments, over 8 to 12 weeks of daily use.

What’s debated

A 2023 review of 26 trials found benefits. A 2025 review found none in trials without industry funding.

Questions.

Still deciding? Book a free consultation with our nutrition team.

How many jars do I need?

One 200 g jar holds about 25 daily scoops, roughly 3½ weeks. A full 12-week course is about 3½ jars, so a pack of 4 covers it with a little to spare.

How long before I notice anything?

Most studies ran for 8 to 12 weeks of daily use, so give it at least 8 weeks before deciding. Results vary from person to person.

Can I mix it into hot drinks?

Yes. It dissolves in coffee, tea and soups without changing the taste.

Who shouldn’t take it?

It’s made from fish, so it isn’t suitable if you’re allergic to fish or vegetarian. If you’re pregnant or breastfeeding, check with your doctor first.
